Node, GPU type, cluster job and memory are recorded when the job runs (memory as reported by the cluster at the end of the run). For jobs run before that, they are taken from the LSF report at the end of run.out, if any (cluster jobs). CPUs and GPUs in italic are the requested ones (job.script or job parameters), not recorded at run time; '-' is not available. CPU and GPU hours are the CPUs and GPUs of each job times its elapsed time. Jobs run locally show their pid (the schedule watcher one while scheduled) and, without a cluster report, their max RSS when profiled. Usage is the average / maximum of the last profiled run (EMWRAP_PROFILE or the job 'Profile' param). Click a job to see its details and resource usage over time.
